Worth the climb.

## The Trail

It starts at the base of a hill, past the kind of road you'd drive by without a second thought.

The path is all roots and rocks — the kind of trail that keeps your eyes down. No handrails. No paved steps. Just the mountain, doing what mountains do.

## The Top

Sugarcane fields below. The reef beyond. January sky, clear all the way to the horizon.

No crowds. No signs pointing you here. Just Ishigaki, from the top.

## January in Ishigaki

Winter here doesn't feel like winter. The air is sharp, the colors stay green, and the sea holds its blue longer than it has any right to.

Come in January. Hike this. You won't regret it.
