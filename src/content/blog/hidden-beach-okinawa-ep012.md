## How It Stays Hidden

The beach that everyone goes to is well-documented. Parking lot. Vending machines. Rental equipment. Reviewed by ten thousand people on Google Maps.

Not on any tourist map. Almost no one finds it. Not because it's hard to reach — but because nobody looks.

## What's There

No signs. No people. No rental chairs. No one selling shaved ice.

Just the water. Just the sand. Just the sound.

The kind of beach that makes you wonder why you spent three days at the resort.
