## Up Here.

Most people are down there — in the streets, at the port, looking for lunch.

Up here, it's different.

Sky Bar by BATIDA sits above the city, with an infinity pool stretching toward the sea. The kind of place you walk into and immediately understand why people don't leave.

## The Pool. The Drinks.

The bar counter runs along the edge of the pool. You can sit in the water or stay dry — either way, the drinks come to you.

The sea is right there. Not in the background. Right in front of you.

In Ishigaki, even the bars face the ocean.

## And a Sauna.

There's a sauna, too.

Wood panels, soft light, and a window that looks out onto the terrace. The kind of sauna that makes sense after a swim, before another drink.

Ishigaki has levels.

## The Short Version

An infinity pool. A bar by the water. A sauna above the city.

Sky Bar by BATIDA, Ishigaki.
