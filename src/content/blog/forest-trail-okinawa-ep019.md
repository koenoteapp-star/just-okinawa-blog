## Just a Road.

No sign. No tour bus. No one to tell you this is worth stopping for.

It's a road. A coastal path cutting through the grasslands — the kind that appears on no tourist map, that no guide book mentions, that most people drive past without a second thought.

And yet.

The grass opens wide. The path stretches out. And there's the sky above, and the sea right there beside you — like the island is just showing you everything at once.

## The Thing About Okinawa

The famous spots in Okinawa are worth seeing. The beaches, the markets, the castles.

But the places that stay with you are often the ones you weren't looking for.

A road along the coast. An afternoon with nowhere to be. A direction you chose for no particular reason.

That's how you find this.

## What's Out There

The coastal road runs along the edges of Okinawa's main island — open grassland on one side, the sea stretching out on the other.

There are no facilities. No parking lot with a souvenir stand. You walk, the path unfolds, and eventually you understand why people keep coming back to this island even when they think they've already seen it.

They haven't.
