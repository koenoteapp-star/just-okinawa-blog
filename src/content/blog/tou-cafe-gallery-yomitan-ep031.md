A cafe in Yomitan where the coffee is good, the cheesecake is better, and the pottery on the shelves is for sale.

---

## The Space

tou is not trying to be anything.

Concrete walls. Handmade pottery. A wooden chest with a ceramic vase on top. The kind of place that feels put together without feeling designed.

## The Food

The cheesecake comes on a plate made in the same building. The coffee is cold, layered, served without explanation.

Both are worth ordering.

## The Pottery

The shelves aren't decoration. Everything is for sale — cups, plates, small pieces you'd actually use at home.

It's a gallery that happens to serve coffee, or a cafe that happens to sell art. Either way, you leave with something.

## Getting There

Yomitan is about 90 minutes from Naha Airport by car. tou is easy to miss — look for the sign at the entrance.

Go on a weekday if you can. The space is small and fills up.
