## A River.

No sign pointing to it. No name on the map that anyone bothers with.

Just water moving slowly through green. Mangrove roots at the edge. The kind of place you find by not looking for anything in particular.

In Okinawa, these spots exist everywhere. You just have to be moving slowly enough to notice them.

## The Grass. The Tide.

A few steps further and the grass opens up.

The tide coming in. The ocean just past the rocks. The kind of view that doesn't photograph well — you have to be there, close enough to hear it, close enough to feel the salt in the air.

Most people walk straight to the beach. They miss this part.

## Golden Hour.

Then the light changed.

It happens fast in Okinawa. One moment it's afternoon. The next, the sky is doing something you didn't plan for.

We stayed. There wasn't anywhere else to be.

## The Short Version

A river. The grass. The tide. Then golden hour.

Okinawa at its quietest.
