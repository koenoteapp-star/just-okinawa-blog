Cliffs. A lighthouse. The sun dropping into the ocean.

Uganzaki doesn't need much explanation. You show up, and it does the rest.

---

## The Western Edge

Uganzaki is on the western tip of Ishigaki Island — a cape that juts out toward the setting sun, with cliffs that fall straight into the East China Sea.

Most visitors to Ishigaki head for Kabira Bay or the beaches near town. Uganzaki is a different kind of stop — quieter, more open, and best in the late afternoon when the light starts to change.

## What It Looks Like

A white lighthouse on a ridge above black volcanic rock. Grass that bends in the wind. Below, the water is deep and clear, and the cliffs are sharp enough that you stay back from the edge.

As the sun gets lower, everything changes color. The rock, the grass, the sea. It happens slowly and then all at once.

Not many people are there when it's happening.

## Getting There

Uganzaki is about 30 minutes west of Ishigaki City by car. There's a small parking area. The walk to the cape is short.

Go late in the day.
