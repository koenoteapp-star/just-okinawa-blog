Water buffaloes were brought to Yaeyama centuries ago to work the land. Most islands moved on. Taketomi didn't.

---

## The Cart

You hear it before you see it. The slow clop of hooves on coral stone, the creak of wooden wheels.

The cart seats maybe eight people. A driver sits at the front, playing three-line shamisen. Nobody talks much. There's not much to say.

## The Lanes

Taketomi's lanes are narrow — flanked by walls of stacked coral rock, low enough to see the hibiscus over the top. The buffalo sets the pace, and the pace is slow.

This is not a problem.

## Getting There

Taketomi is a 10-minute ferry from Ishigaki. The water buffalo tours run from the village center — you can't miss them.

Go in the morning before the tour groups arrive.
