There's a seafood restaurant in Naha that most visitors never find.

鮮魚 ふくむら.

---

## What It Is

A small restaurant. Run by a grandmother and grandfather. The kind of place that has been here longer than most people have been eating out.

No website. No reservations. You walk in, you sit down, you eat.

## The Food

Fresh catch. Every day. Piled onto rice or served as a set meal with miso soup and pickles.

The seafood is the point. Not the atmosphere. Not the service. Just whatever came in that morning, prepared simply, and priced like it was still 1995.

Under ¥1,000.

## Where It Is

Naha. The kind of location that filters out anyone who isn't looking for it.

## The Short Version

Fresh catch. Piled high. Under ¥1,000. Run by a grandmother and grandfather.

This is what lunch looks like in Naha.
