Zamami Island sits in the middle of the Kerama Islands. The water is clear enough to see the bottom. The town is quiet enough to hear the wind.

---

## The Ferry

The high-speed ferry leaves Tomari Port in Naha. About 90 minutes later, you're somewhere that feels completely different.

There's no buildup. One moment you're in a city of half a million people. The next, you're watching the harbor shrink behind you as the sea turns that specific shade of blue.

## The Water

The Kerama blue is a thing people talk about before they've seen it, and still can't quite explain after.

It's not one color. It shifts — pale aquamarine over the shallows, deep indigo further out. Where the light hits the bottom, it glows.

You don't need to snorkel to see it. You can see it from the ferry. You can see it standing on the pier.

## Getting There

The high-speed ferry from Tomari Port (Naha) takes about 90 minutes to Zamami. Slower ferries also run. Ferries don't always run in rough weather, so check schedules before you go.

Zamami has a few guesthouses and minshuku. Day trips are possible from Naha.
