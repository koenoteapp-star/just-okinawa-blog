A pool. A sauna. Northern Ishigaki.

No schedule. No agenda. Just the light on the water and somewhere to sit while time moves slowly.

---

## The Luxury of Doing Nothing

There's a version of travel that's about checking things off. Beaches visited, snorkel spots hit, sunset photos taken.

And then there's this.

A pool in the north of Ishigaki Island, far enough from the tourist center that the only sound is the water. A barrel sauna sitting in the garden. Lounge chairs that nobody is rushing you off of.

The luxury here isn't the amenities. It's the permission to stay put.

## Why Ibaruma

Ibaruma is in the northern part of Ishigaki — past the airport, past most of the hotels, up toward the quieter end of the island where the road narrows and the pace changes on its own.

Most visitors to Ishigaki stay near Kabira Bay or in Ishigaki City. The north has fewer people, fewer signs telling you where to go, and more of the Ishigaki that was there before anyone was telling you to visit it.

This kind of place exists up there. A pool, a sauna, and time that belongs to you.

## Getting There

Ibaruma is about 30 minutes north of Ishigaki City by car. A rental car is the easiest way — public buses run infrequently on this route.

The north of Ishigaki rewards the people who make it that far.
